# Product Edition Plan

## Product

Working name: **ShortsPilot — AI Shorts Production Kit**

Positioning: a beginner-friendly, self-hosted toolkit for creating narrated YouTube Shorts with AI, without requiring a paid automation platform subscription.

## What we are selling

We are **not** selling a claim of guaranteed YouTube growth, monetization, or passive income.

The paid value is:

- easier setup than the raw developer repository
- beginner-oriented configuration and preflight checks
- curated niche/topic/prompt packs
- reusable Shorts templates
- clear troubleshooting and recovery guides
- demo mode and sample configuration
- packaging that a non-developer can follow
- optional paid installation/setup service later

## Licensing rule

The underlying project is MIT licensed. Preserve the existing LICENSE and upstream attribution. Do not remove or hide the original author attribution. Our original additions can be separately identified, but the product must not falsely claim the upstream code as our original work.

## Target first customer

A non-technical creator who wants to automate production of faceless Shorts but does not want to learn Node.js, APIs, FFmpeg, OAuth, prompt engineering, and YouTube publishing details.

## First offer

**Starter — ₹499 launch price**

Includes:
- Product Edition code/package
- beginner setup guide
- Gemini-first setup path where supported
- 10 niche starter packs
- 50+ reusable prompts
- preflight checklist
- troubleshooting guide
- sample configuration
- demo workflow

**Setup Service — ₹1,999 launch price**

We install/configure the product remotely and help the buyer generate their first working Short. No promise of channel growth or monetization.

## Build order

1. Product documentation and attribution notice
2. Beginner setup/preflight experience
3. Niche and prompt packs
4. Demo/sample assets
5. Packaging and release ZIP
6. Product landing page/listing
7. Outreach to first prospects
8. Use buyer questions to improve the product

## Validation rule

Do not spend weeks polishing before testing demand. Get the first listing and outreach live as soon as the package is genuinely usable. If nobody responds, change the offer/target/customer problem before adding more features.
