# ShortsPilot Niche Starter Packs

Use these as starting points, not as guarantees of demand or growth. Each pack is designed to produce useful short-form educational content without relying on copyrighted clips.

## 1. Developer Tips
Audience: junior developers and career switchers.
Content: debugging habits, Git tips, API basics, interview mistakes.

## 2. AI Productivity
Audience: knowledge workers and developers.
Content: practical workflows, prompt patterns, automation ideas, tool comparisons.

## 3. Personal Finance Education
Audience: young professionals.
Content: budgeting concepts, compounding, emergency funds, common money mistakes.
Avoid personalised financial advice or promises.

## 4. Productivity Systems
Audience: students and professionals.
Content: planning systems, focus methods, note-taking, workflow experiments.

## 5. Tech Explainers
Audience: non-technical viewers curious about technology.
Content: APIs, databases, cloud concepts, cybersecurity basics, how apps work.

## 6. Software Career
Audience: early-career developers.
Content: portfolio mistakes, resume structure, interview preparation, learning plans.

## 7. History Facts
Audience: general-interest viewers.
Content: short historical events, inventions, timelines, surprising but sourced facts.
Use reliable sources and avoid fabricated facts.

## 8. Science Explained
Audience: curious general viewers.
Content: everyday physics, biology, space, computing concepts.
Keep claims evidence-based.

## 9. Business Case Studies
Audience: founders, students, professionals.
Content: product decisions, business models, failures and lessons.
Clearly distinguish facts from interpretation.

## 10. Everyday How-To
Audience: broad consumer audience.
Content: practical software, phone, computer and digital-workflow tips.
Avoid unsafe instructions and unsupported claims.

## Topic selection rule

Prefer evergreen questions that a viewer can understand in 30–60 seconds. A good topic should have a clear hook, one useful takeaway, and enough factual material to support the script.

## Content quality rule

Do not invent statistics, quotes, testimonials, earnings, scientific claims or news. If a number is important, verify it before publishing.
