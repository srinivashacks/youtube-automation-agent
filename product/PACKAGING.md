# ShortsPilot Product Packaging

## What the buyer is getting

ShortsPilot is a beginner-oriented packaging of an open-source YouTube automation project. It is intended to reduce setup friction for creators who want a repeatable AI-assisted Shorts workflow.

### Included

- beginner setup path
- preflight checklist
- niche starter ideas
- reusable prompt library
- sample configuration
- troubleshooting guidance
- original product documentation
- upstream MIT license and attribution

### Not promised

- YouTube views
- subscribers
- monetization approval
- revenue
- passive income
- guaranteed channel growth

## Buyer safety

API providers and YouTube may change their policies, quotas, pricing, or available models. Buyers are responsible for their own accounts, API usage, content rights, and compliance with platform rules.
